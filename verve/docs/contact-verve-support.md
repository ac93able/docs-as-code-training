# Contact Verve Support

Use this page when you have already reviewed the relevant guidance and still need help with a Verve issue. Start with the most relevant troubleshooting topic, then contact your administrator or support team with the details below.

## When to contact support

Contact support when:

- a sign-in, startup, installation, or project access problem continues after you follow the troubleshooting steps
- the issue affects more than one user, project, or environment
- the problem appears to be caused by a system setting, deployment issue, or account problem that requires administrator action
- you need help interpreting error messages, missing permissions, or startup behavior

## Before you contact support

Collect the information that helps the investigation move quickly:

1. Confirm the problem is reproducible and note the exact workflow that fails.
2. Record the expected result and the actual behavior you observed.
3. Save the exact error message, warning, or screen behavior you saw.
4. List the steps you already tried and the result of each step.
5. Include the Verve version, operating system, time of failure, and affected account or project.

## What to include in your request

Use the support record below when you contact your administrator or support team:

```yaml
problem: ""
expected_result: ""
message_displayed: ""
steps_already_tried: []
verve_version: ""
operating_system: ""
time_of_failure: ""
affected_user_project_or_account: ""
```

:::warning
Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or other secrets in a support request.
:::

## Next step

After you send the request, keep the message, screenshot, and version details together so your administrator or support team can compare changes across the investigation. If the issue affects multiple users, include that information early so the team can assess whether the issue is isolated or system-wide.
