Use this guide when you cannot see a project, open a project, or complete a project task in Verve.

## Before you get started

- Confirm that you can sign in to Verve.
- Identify the project you are trying to open.
- Contact a project owner or administrator if you need access changed.

## Check project access

1. Open the Projects page.
2. Search for the project by name.
3. Open the project if it appears in the results.
4. Contact the project owner if the project does not appear.

You can work in the project when it appears and your account has the required role.

## Resolve a permission problem

1. Ask the project owner or administrator to review your membership.
2. Ask the administrator to confirm that your role allows the task.
3. Sign out and sign in again after your access changes.

Your access is ready when you can open the project and complete the required task.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can I sign in but not see a project?

Your account may not be a project member, or the project may not be visible to your role. Ask the project owner or administrator to review your access.

### Who can change project access?

Project owners or administrators can update membership and roles in [Manage Roles](../administration-guide/manage-roles.md).

## Best Practices

- Use clear project names so users can find the correct project.
- Assign only the access each user needs.
- Review project membership when a user's responsibilities change.
