# Troubleshoot sign-in problems

Use this guide when Verve does not accept your credentials or does not let you access your account.

## Before you get started

- Confirm that you know your Verve username.
- Have access to the email address or support contact associated with your account.
- Ask an administrator to confirm your account status if you are unsure whether it is active.

## Check your credentials

1. Open Verve.
2. Enter your username exactly as it was provided.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If Verve accepts your credentials, sign-in is complete.

## Resolve an account problem

1. Confirm that your account is active.
2. Ask an administrator to reset your password if you cannot verify it.
3. Try signing in again after the administrator confirms the account change.

You can access Verve after your credentials are correct and your account is active.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if I forgot my password?

Contact your administrator and ask for a password reset. Do not share your current password.

### Why can another user sign in while I cannot?

Your account may be inactive, or your credentials may differ from the other user's credentials. Ask an administrator to verify your account.

## Best Practices

- Enter your username and password carefully.
- Do not share your password with other users.
- Contact an administrator promptly when your account status changes.
