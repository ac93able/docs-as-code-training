# Troubleshoot startup problems

Use this guide when Verve does not open or closes before you can sign in.

## Before you get started

- Confirm that Verve is installed on the device.
- Save your work in other applications before restarting the device.
- Have administrator contact information available if the problem continues.

## Restart Verve

1. Close Verve if it is open.
2. Open Verve again.
3. Wait for the sign-in screen to appear.

If the sign-in screen appears, Verve has started successfully.

## Restart the device

1. Close other applications.
2. Restart the device.
3. Open Verve after the device finishes restarting.

If Verve still does not start, contact your administrator or support team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if Verve opens and then closes?

Restart the device and open Verve again. If the problem continues, record the behavior and contact your administrator.

### What should I do if Verve shows a blank screen?

Close and reopen Verve. If the blank screen remains, contact your administrator and describe when it appears.

## Best Practices

- Keep Verve updated according to your organization's maintenance process.
- Restart Verve after an installation or configuration change.
- Record error messages before closing the application.
